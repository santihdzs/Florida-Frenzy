FLORIDA FRENZY

Florida Frenzy is a 2D roguelite that fuses the strategy of a TCG with the adrenaline of a platformer.

Assume the role of the Crock Clan and survive the threats of the swamp. Collect cards, gain experience, and participate in duels that scale in difficulty.

Experiment with a dynamic gameplay that combines familiar mechanics like UNO and classic Pokémon TCG attributes, in short but intense 'runs'.

The Differentiating Element: A dual system of Elemental Energy and Instinct. It's not enough to simply "match" by number or element; each tactical decision counts towards mastering the ecosystem.
